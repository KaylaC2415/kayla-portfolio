01

Overview

As Five Mics’ player base and content library grew, information became fragmented across platforms. New players struggled to find a starting point, while existing players had to search across separate sources.

I led the redesign and development of a central hub connecting tutorials, the card database, organized play, lore, events, news, and retailer information.

02

Key Challenges

Organize a growing ecosystem

Give rules, card references, retailer resources, and community updates a structure that can grow with the game.

Guide new players

Create a clear starting point across tutorials, rules, card types, and gameplay mechanics.

Make content discoverable

Help players explore related cards, abilities, lore, events, and resources across pages.

03

IA Planning

I grouped the site around player tasks—learning the game, exploring cards and lore, finding events, and buying cards—so rules, card references, community activity, and retailer resources connect within one navigable ecosystem rather than remain isolated destinations.

04

Design & Development

Express the game’s personality

I designed and built the site in Framer, using motion, visual storytelling, and dynamic homepage sections to introduce the game and encourage exploration.

Support a growing card database

I developed custom code components and overrides for advanced card filtering. The resulting site combines marketing, education, and community resources in one experience.

Results

50%Increase in site visits

Site visits in the first month after launch were 50% higher than the previous website’s average.

  • Organized hundreds of collectible cards into a searchable, filterable database.
  • Built custom filtering components to support content discovery.
  • Established an information architecture that can accommodate future expansions.
  • Created a dedicated onboarding path for new players.
