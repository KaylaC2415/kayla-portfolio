Five Mics Mobile TCG

A mobile trading card game bringing hip-hop culture into play

Designed and implemented the UI/UX for a hip-hop-inspired mobile trading card game, redesigning onboarding, building social features, and creating core gameplay interfaces with a bold visual language, bringing interactions to life in Unity with a focus on clarity and player engagement.

Project Objectives

Goal

  • Adapt desktop gameplay for mobile with a seamless interface.
  • Enhance UI accessibility and visual appeal.
  • Design intuitive onboarding for new players.
  • Strengthen social features to boost player interaction.

Challenge

Design a clear and intuitive interface to guide players while addressing the visual presentation of complex interactions.

Responsibilities

  • UI/UX Design - Transforming physical to digital
  • Visual Direction - Setting visual style and vibe
  • Implementation - Hand-off with Unity UI and interactions built by C#
02

Visual Direction

Starting with visual keywords from the founder, I explored mood boards and narrowed the direction to a balance between cyberpunk futurism and graffiti-inspired street culture. Referencing games like Marvel Snap, I extracted a focused palette of bold, saturated colors grounded by dark neutrals to capture the energy of hip-hop while keeping the UI clear and cohesive.

05

Unity Implementation

After iterating on the UI with the team, I implemented the responsive interfaces in Unity, including button animations, scene transitions, and drag-and-drop interactions. I also created custom shaders for holographic card upgrades to enhance visual feedback and reinforce the game’s visual identity.

06

Result & Feedback

30%Higher Tutorial Completion Rate
20%Increase in Returning Users

Players praised the eye-catching visuals and seamless interactions. During a two-week playtest, tutorial completion rate improved by 30%. Surveys, playtests, and analytics informed three targeted iterations to address usability issues and improve the player experience.

Each change reflected our commitment to delivering a polished, player-first experience.
